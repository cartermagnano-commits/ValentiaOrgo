Step 2 of 3 — Transition State
The nucleophile (OH⁻) attacks the back of the electrophilic carbon, forming a pentacoordinate transition state. The C–Br bond stretches and weakens as the C–O bond begins to form.
✓ Complete — SN2 proceeds via a single concerted step. Configuration inverts (Walden inversion). No carbocation intermediate.

Key Facts

SN2 is a concerted substitution — bond formation and breaking are simultaneous, giving clean stereochemical inversion.

Rate Law

Second-order overall. Both concentrations affect the rate.
